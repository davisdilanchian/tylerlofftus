Storyline

The Tyler Loftus Show is the longest-running television series in the history of the planet. It was shot entirely on the real FX soundstage with a live studio audience. The role of Tyler was played by George Lopez until 2006, when he was recast as Benicio del Toro. Until the year 2025, no copies of the show existed; they were only rediscovered in a storage unit, having been purchased at auction by Kevin Loftus (no relation).

Trivia

  • Early VFX artist George Lucas got his start working on The Tyler Loftus Show, CGI-ing Tyler Loftus's famous hat after it was mysteriously lost during production.
  • The famous scene where Tyler screams out loud after kicking a helmet was actually the actor himself breaking his toe, coincidentally after kicking the helmet.
  • The rivalry between Tyler and Dustin is based on the real-life rivalry between Mozart and Salieri.
  • Tyler Loftus was not based on a real human being. He was more mytho-historical in nature, scholars believe.

Did you know
